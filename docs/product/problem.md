# StudySync — Primary Product Problem

## 🔍 Working Problem Hypothesis

> *"People can create individual study plans, but when multiple people study together, there is no simple shared timeline that helps everyone know what they should be doing now, what comes next, and how the group is progressing together."*

*(Note: This is a working hypothesis, not validated market research.)*

---

## 🛑 The Friction Today

When study partners or small groups attempt to collaborate or co-focus remotely:

1. **Disconnected Planning Tools:** Plans live in Notion, Google Docs, or Discord text messages where they are static and separate from the timer.
2. **Unsynchronized Timers:** Each participant runs their own Pomodoro timer or clock, leading to desynchronized breaks, interrupted focus, and confusion.
3. **Ambiguity of Active Task:** Participants frequently ask *"What are we working on right now?"* or *"How much time do we have left on this section?"*
4. **Heavyweight Alternatives:** Existing project management tools (Trello, Jira, Asana) are built for asynchronous project tracking, not real-time, minute-by-minute study execution.

---

## 💡 The Proposed Solution

StudySync unifies **timeline planning** with **real-time synchronized execution**:
- A single visual horizontal timeline that everyone can see.
- An authoritative timer that counts down simultaneously for all participants.
- Clear visual indicators of the active block, upcoming breaks, and session progress.

# StudySync — Problem Definition & Hypothesis

## Primary Product Problem Hypothesis

> **"People can create individual study plans, but when multiple people study together, there is no simple shared timeline that helps everyone know what they should be doing now, what comes next, and how the group is progressing together."**

---

## Important Distinction

* **This is a hypothesis, NOT a proven fact.**
* Do not present this hypothesis as validated market research.
* We test this hypothesis continuously by using the prototype ourselves, testing with study partners, and observing real collaborative study sessions.

---

## Problem Breakdown & Friction Points

### 1. Lack of Shared Pacing & Structure
When studying together over Discord, Zoom, or in-person, group members often work on separate schedules, lose track of time, or disagree on when breaks should occur.

### 2. Low Visibility into Active Tasks
Traditional Pomodoro timers or shared text notes don't clearly display:
- What task block is active *right now*
- What task block comes *next*
- How much time remains in the current block
- Who is currently focusing vs paused

### 3. Setup & Coordination Friction
Existing task managers require tedious board configurations, or lack real-time synchronized timers. Collaborative document tools lack visual horizontal timelines tied directly to active countdown timers.

---

## How StudySync Solves The Problem

StudySync combines a **visual shared timeline** with **real-time synchronized timer execution**, delivering absolute clarity for every room participant:

1. **What is happening now**
2. **What comes next**
3. **How much time remains**
4. **Who is in the room**
5. **Whether the session is running or paused**
6. **What has been completed**
