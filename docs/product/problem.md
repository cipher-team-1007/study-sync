# StudySync — Problem Definition & Hypothesis

## 🔍 Primary Product Problem Hypothesis

> **"People can create individual study plans, but when multiple people study together, there is no simple shared timeline that helps everyone know what they should be doing now, what comes next, and how the group is progressing together."**

---

## ⚠️ Important Distinction

* **This is a working hypothesis, NOT a proven fact or validated market research.**
* We test this hypothesis continuously by using the prototype ourselves, testing with study partners, and observing real collaborative study sessions.

---

## 🛑 Problem Breakdown & Friction Today

When study partners or small groups attempt to collaborate or co-focus remotely:

### 1. Disconnected Planning & Static Notes
Plans live in Notion, Google Docs, or Discord text messages where they are static and completely separated from the timer.

### 2. Lack of Shared Pacing & Unsynchronized Timers
Each participant runs their own Pomodoro timer or clock, leading to desynchronized breaks, interrupted focus, and confusion.

### 3. Ambiguity of Active Tasks
Traditional Pomodoro timers or shared text notes don't clearly display:
- What task block is active *right now*
- What task block comes *next*
- How much time remains in the current block
- Who is currently focusing vs paused

### 4. Heavyweight Alternatives & Setup Friction
Existing project management tools (Trello, Jira, Asana) are built for asynchronous project tracking, not real-time, minute-by-minute study execution. Collaborative document tools lack visual horizontal timelines tied directly to active countdown timers.

---

## 💡 How StudySync Solves The Problem

StudySync combines a **visual shared timeline** with **real-time synchronized timer execution**, delivering absolute clarity for every room participant:

1. **What is happening now** (active focus block / break)
2. **What comes next** (upcoming schedule on the visual timeline)
3. **How much time remains** (authoritative synchronized countdown)
4. **Who is in the room** (presence and participant visibility)
5. **Whether the session is running or paused** (realtime synchronized state)
6. **What has been completed** (shared milestone review and progress)
