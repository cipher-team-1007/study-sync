# StudySync — Product Non-Goals

To maintain strict product focus and prevent scope creep, this document outlines what StudySync is **deliberately NOT trying to become**.

---

## 🎯 Distinguishing Anti-Audiences vs. Product Non-Goals

- **Anti-Audiences** define *who we are not serving* right now (e.g., Enterprise teams, 50-student classrooms).
- **Product Non-Goals** define *what the product will deliberately not build or replace*, even for our primary target users.

---

## 🚫 Explicit Product Non-Goals

### 1. NOT a General Task Manager
StudySync is not a replacement for Notion, Todoist, Asana, or Jira. We are not building backlog grooming, hierarchical project sub-tasks, multi-week sprint planners, or complex task assignment boards. StudySync is strictly for **in-session time-blocking and live execution**.

### 2. NOT a Replacement for Google Calendar / Life Schedulers
StudySync is designed for focused study blocks (e.g., a 2-hour Saturday afternoon session), not all-day personal calendar management, recurring calendar invite systems, or scheduling appointment slots with external parties.

### 3. NOT a Video Calling Platform
We are deliberately **not** building native video/audio calls or screen-sharing into StudySync. Users already communicate effectively via Discord, Zoom, FaceTime, Google Meet, or side-by-side in real life. StudySync is built to run cleanly **alongside** existing communication channels without forcing users to abandon their preferred voice tools.

### 4. NOT a Social Network or Public Feed
StudySync will not include public activity feeds, friend request systems, follower graphs, leaderboards, or global discovery directories. Sessions are private collaborative spaces for invited peers.

### 5. NOT a Music Streaming Service
StudySync will not build embedded audio players or license music streaming services (Spotify/Apple Music integrations). Users can listen to their own music or study playlists independently while staying synchronized to the room timeline.

### 6. NOT an AI-First Productivity Assistant
StudySync will not position AI as the core driver of the experience. The product value comes from human peers committing to a shared visual timeline and executing together, not autonomous agents generating schedules.

---

## 🧭 Why Non-Goals Matter

Every feature we decline to build protects the speed, simplicity, and rock-solid reliability of our core value:
> **Creating a shared visual timeline and executing it together with synchronized focus.**
