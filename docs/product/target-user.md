# StudySync — Target User & Audience Strategy

## Primary Target Audience (MVP Focus)

Our initial target audience is tightly focused to optimize core product value and rapid feedback:

- **Study Partners**: 2 people studying together remotely or side-by-side.
- **College / University Students**: Preparing for exams, working on group study blocks.
- **Exam Preparation Groups**: Small peer groups studying for structured exams (e.g. SAT, MCAT, GRE, finals).
- **Small Study Groups**: 2-5 participants seeking synchronized focus sessions.

---

## Target Persona: "The Focused Peer Pair"

* **Needs**: Clear agenda, mutual accountability, synchronized breaks, zero setup overhead.
* **Pain Points**: Losing focus during study calls, forgetting break intervals, constant text messaging asking "are we starting now?".
* **Goal**: Join a room, see a clear visual schedule, press Start, and focus together.

---

## Explicit Anti-Audiences (Not Served in MVP)

To avoid feature creep and bloated scope, we explicitly do **NOT** attempt to serve the following during early MVP development:

| Anti-Audience | Reason for Delay / Rejection |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise / Corporate Teams** | Requires complex role-based access, SSO, compliance, multi-tenant billing. |
| **Teachers & Large Classrooms** | Requires lecture modes, monitoring dashboards, grade integration, 50+ student scaling. |
| **Gamers / Social Networks** | Requires heavy social feeds, match-making, avatars, complex gamification ecosystems. |
| **Families & Home Management** | Requires shared calendars, chore lists, notification hubs outside core study context. |

---

## Experience Principles

StudySync experiences must feel:
* **Calm & Focused**
* **Collaborative & Synchronized**
* **Clear & Lightweight**
* **Intentional & Human**

StudySync MUST NOT feel:
* Bloated or cluttered
* Corporate or administrative
* Overly gamified or distracting
* Overly AI-driven
