# StudySync — Target User & Audience Strategy

## 🎯 Primary vs. Secondary Target Audience

To maximize clarity and reduce collaboration complexity during early product development, our audience is structured as:

### Primary Target (MVP Core)
* **2 Study Partners**: Two students who regularly study together and want a shared structure without staying on a call just to coordinate.

### Secondary Target (Post-Validation)
* **3–5 Person Study Groups**: Small peer groups preparing for structured exams (e.g., SAT, MCAT, GRE, university finals).

---

## 💡 Why Focus Strictly on 2 Study Partners for MVP?

Designing for **Person A + Person B** is significantly cleaner than designing for **A + B + C + D + E**:

1. **Simpler Presence & State**: Tracking two active connections avoids large presence rosters and heavy connection overhead.
2. **Clear Ownership**: A 2-person dynamic naturally maps to Host (owner/controller) and Peer (participant), avoiding complex multi-user voting, granular permissions, or concurrent editing conflicts.
3. **Focused UX**: Screen real estate and notification states remain minimal and calm.

---

## 👤 Target Persona: "The Focused Peer Pair"

* **Who**: Alex and Maya, two university students taking the same demanding course who frequently study together remotely or in the library.
* **Needs**: Clear agreed-upon agenda, mutual pacing/accountability, synchronized rest breaks, zero setup overhead.
* **Pain Points**: Losing focus during study calls, forgetting break intervals, constant text messaging asking *"are we starting now?"* or *"how much time is left on this chapter?"*.
* **Goal**: One person creates a session link, sends it over chat, the friend opens it, and both immediately see the shared plan and start focusing in unison.

---

## 🚫 Explicit Anti-Audiences (Not Served in MVP)

| Anti-Audience | Reason for Exclusion |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise / Corporate Teams** | Requires complex role-based access, SSO, compliance, multi-tenant billing, meeting software integrations. |
| **Teachers & Large Classrooms (20–50+ students)** | Requires teacher administrative dashboards, student attendance tracking, grading tools, and high-concurrency broadcast architecture. |
| **Gamers / Public Social Networks** | Requires public discovery feeds, match-making, avatar stores, and complex gamification ecosystems. |
| **Families & General Home Management** | Requires shared chore calendars, shopping lists, and notifications outside focused study sessions. |

---

## 🎨 Experience Principles

StudySync experiences must feel:
* **Calm & Focused**
* **Collaborative & Synchronized**
* **Clear & Lightweight**
* **Intentional & Human**

StudySync MUST NOT feel:
* Bloated or cluttered
* Corporate or administrative
* Overly gamified or noisy
* Overly AI-driven
