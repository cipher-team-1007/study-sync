# StudySync — Target User & Audience Strategy

## 🎯 Primary Target Audience (MVP)

Our initial focus for MVP development is tight and specific:

- **Study Partners:** Pairs of students or peers co-studying for tests, assignments, or certifications.
- **College Students:** Peers working on shared study sessions or parallel coursework.
- **Exam Preparation Groups:** Small cohorts (2–5 people) studying for standardized tests or finals.

---

## 🚫 Out-of-Scope Audiences (Do NOT Target in MVP)

To avoid feature bloat and preserve clarity, we are intentionally **not** targeting:
- K-12 Classrooms / Teachers (no grading, assignment submissions, or LMS integrations)
- Corporate Enterprises / Companies (no SSO, billing tiers, or organization hierarchies)
- Families / Casual Home Users (no parent-child permissions)
- Large Gaming Guilds / Discord Communities (no huge voice lobbies or bot ecosystems)

---

## 👤 Primary Persona Archetype: "The Study Buddy"

- **Profile:** College student studying with a peer over video call or side-by-side.
- **Goal:** Agree on what they need to accomplish in a 2-hour window, break it into 40-minute focus blocks and 10-minute breaks, and stay accountable without constantly checking the clock.
- **Frustration:** Constantly asking *"Did your timer start?"*, *"When is break time?"*, or losing track of tasks.
- **Magic Moment:** Pasting the room URL to their friend, seeing their friend instantly appear, and having the active study block count down on both screens synchronously.
